  <h1>Privacy Policy for “Budgets”</h1>


  <p><strong>Last updated: 08/02/2026</strong></p>


  <p>Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how the “Budgets” app (the “App”) handles your data.</p>


  <h2>1. Core principle: no data collection</h2>

  <p>We (the developer) do not collect, store, or process any personal data on our own servers. We do not operate any servers for this App.</p>


  <h2>2. Local storage</h2>

  <p>All financial data, categories, and settings are stored <strong>locally on your device only</strong>.</p>


  <h2>3. Sync (Multipeer Connectivity)</h2>

  <p>The App can sync data between nearby devices.</p>

  <ul>

    <li>This uses Apple’s <strong>Multipeer Connectivity</strong> framework.</li>

    <li>Data is transferred directly from device to device via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.</li>

    <li>Data does not leave your local network and is not sent over the internet to external servers.</li>

  </ul>


  <h2>4. In-app purchases</h2>

  <p>Purchases (for example, “Full Version”) are processed exclusively through the Apple App Store (StoreKit).</p>

  <ul>

    <li>We only receive an anonymised confirmation from Apple that a purchase was successful.</li>

    <li>We do not have access to your payment details, name, or billing address.</li>

  </ul>
